AhaSlides visual identity: colour rules

These rules replace the colour section of the current brand guidelines. Everything not mentioned here (the font, spacing, components, icons) stays as it is in the design system.

Last updated 8 October 2026

Design system home: ahaslides-product.github.io/ahaslides-design. The tokens the product uses today are on the Colour token reference.

The rules at a glance

  1. Vivid Pink (#E70E68) is the one primary colour. Purple is the logo only, plus one small playful touch on white.
  2. Page backgrounds are white, and the first screen is white. Two coloured sections never sit next to each other, and at most two of them are Vivid Pink.
  3. Social, ads, slides, print and video use white or Vivid Pink backgrounds. Nothing else.
  4. Splash backgrounds come in three layouts only: centre, right and minimal.
  5. Pink text is for white only. On Vivid Pink at 5% or on grey, text is black. On Vivid Pink, text is solid white.
  6. No red, yellow, green or blue for status. Use an icon, clear wording and an outline or black fill.
  7. Charts use four colours in order: Vivid Pink, black, Vivid Pink at 30%, light grey.
  8. Illustrations use Vivid Pink tints, Darker Pink, white, the default ink and grey. The 10, 20, 45, 60 and 80% tints never appear in the interface.

Illustrations

2Logo and font

  1. Two versions of the full logo are allowed: the colour logo on white, and the white logo on Vivid Pink.
  2. The logo and its splash icon are unchanged except for one thing: the two hooks that were Radical Pink are now Vivid Pink. The purple hooks and the wordmark stay as they are.
  3. An all-pink logo on a white background is not allowed.
  4. The font is Plus Jakarta Sans, unchanged.
  5. Every product carries the AhaSlides brand: AhaSlides Livelify, AhaSlides Games, AhaSlides Learn, AhaSlides Docs.
  6. Customer and integration logos (Zoom, Claude and others) are shown in one colour: greyscale or Vivid Pink, never in their own brand colours. On a Vivid Pink background they are white. One treatment per row of logos.
  7. Purple appears in the logo. Outside it, purple may be used very sparingly for a dramatic or playful touch: one small decorative element per page, post or slide at most, on white only. It is never used for text people need to read, buttons, links, states, charts or backgrounds, and never in the product app. If unsure, leave it out.

3Backgrounds on the web

  1. The page background is always white.
  2. There are two kinds of coloured section: a Vivid Pink section and a pale pink section (Vivid Pink at 5%). Pale pink sections and boxes are for marketing pages.
  3. Two coloured sections, of either kind, never sit next to each other. A full white section always sits between them; white padding alone does not count.
  4. A page has at most two Vivid Pink sections. Pale pink sections have no limit beyond the rule above.
  5. The first screen is white.
  6. In the product app, light grey is allowed for surfaces that sit inside or beside the white page: cards, sidebars, input fields, table headers and the editor workspace around the slide. Grey is never the background of a whole page or a full-width section.
  7. In the product app, Vivid Pink at 5% is for hover and selected states, plus at most one highlighted panel per screen.
  8. Black backgrounds are allowed in two places only: full-screen presenting, and video or image viewers.
  9. There is no dark mode. People who need a dark screen use their device or browser setting, and our pages must not break under it.

4Backgrounds on other media

  1. Social posts, ads, slides, print and video use white or Vivid Pink as the background. Nothing else.
  2. No grey or black areas.
  3. Black text and the colour logo on white are fine. That is ink, not background.

5Splash backgrounds

A splash background is the splash icon from the logo (its four hooks), enlarged so the frame crops it. Figures below are measured from the vector shapes on pages 4 to 10 of the brand PDF.

  1. Colour: Vivid Pink at 5% on a white background, Darker Pink on a Vivid Pink background. All hooks are one colour.
  2. Three layouts only: centre, right and minimal.
  3. Centre splash. Enlarge the whole icon until it is 4.5 times as wide as the frame's shorter side. Put the point where the four hooks meet at the centre of the frame.
  4. Right splash. Enlarge the whole icon until it is 3.0 times as wide as the frame's shorter side. Put the meeting point 79% of the way across (29% of the width right of centre) and vertically centred.
  5. Minimal splash. Two copies of the largest hook (the lower-left one in the icon), and nothing else. Each copy is 0.77 times the frame's longer side in length. One is turned a quarter-turn anticlockwise and sits in the bottom-left corner. The other is turned a quarter-turn clockwise and sits in the top-right corner. Each is cropped by the frame edge.
  6. Use a splash background on marketing pages, hero areas, empty states, social posts and slides. Never on dense app screens such as the editor.
  7. Centre and right zoom against the frame's shorter side, so the splash stays large in very wide or very tall frames and only parts of the curves show. On a 16:9 frame these figures match the PDF to within 0.5%.
  8. Minimal zooms against the frame's longer side, so the two hooks stay in proportion in wide banners and tall stories alike.

6Buttons, links and text

  1. The primary button is Vivid Pink with white text. On hover it turns Darker Pink and gains a shadow. No underline.
  2. On a Vivid Pink background the primary button is white with Vivid Pink text.
  3. A destructive action (Delete, Remove) is never the primary button. The safe choice (Cancel, Undo) is.
  4. Text on Vivid Pink is solid white, never see-through white.
  5. Text and links on Vivid Pink at 5% or on grey are black. Pink text there is too faint to pass accessibility.
  6. Pink text is allowed on white only.

7Status, alerts and icons

  1. Red, yellow, green and blue are no longer used for danger, warning, success or information.
  2. Show status with an icon, clear wording and an outline or a black fill.
  3. A rise is plain black with an up arrow. A fall is white on a black pill with a down arrow.
  4. File-type icons are one colour.
  5. Borders and shadows use black, white or a palette colour at any transparency.

8Charts

  1. Four colours, in this order: Vivid Pink for the main series, black for the second, Vivid Pink at 30%, then light grey ().
  2. Aim for four series or fewer. The four colours never increase. Beyond four, repeat them with a dashed line or a pattern and write the label beside each mark.
  3. Anything drawn in Vivid Pink at 30% or light grey carries its value in writing, because both are faint on white.
  4. Give each line its own end shape, so the chart still reads in black and white.
  5. Up and down are shown as in section 7, never with green and red.

9What is exempt

  1. Presenter and customer content: slides, uploaded images, their own themes.
  2. Photos.
  3. Official AhaSlides themes and palettes are not exempt. They follow these rules.
  4. Our own illustrations, customer and integration logos and file-type icons are not exempt. They follow these rules.

10The numbers behind the rules

Pair Contrast Verdict
Vivid Pink on white, and white on Vivid Pink 4.51 to 1 Passes for text (needs 4.5)
Purple on white 8.35 to 1 Passes
Purple on Vivid Pink 1.85 to 1 Fails, so purple sits on white only
Darker Pink on white 5.04 to 1 Passes
Vivid Pink text on Vivid Pink at 5% 4.18 to 1 Fails, so text there is black
Vivid Pink text on light grey 4.32 to 1 Fails, so text there is black
See-through white (86%) on Vivid Pink about 3.7 to 1 Fails, so white text is solid
Vivid Pink at 30% against white 1.65 to 1 Faint, so it always carries a written value

For readers with red-green colour blindness, Vivid Pink and a mid grey look almost the same. That is why the chart greys are limited to black and one light grey, and why the tint is 30% and not 35%.
